CUTLINE 0.1.0 developer preview — Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
=======================================================

Video editing without the paywall. Free and open source (MIT).

THIS IS A DEVELOPER PREVIEW of the interface and project tools. It can't
import, play, cut or export video yet; the editing engine arrives in the
next previews.

What works:
  - Welcome screen with YouTube (16:9) and Shorts/TikTok/Reels (9:16) starts
  - Create, open and save .cutline projects; recent projects
  - Sequence settings (resolution presets, frame rates, timecode start)
  - Tracks: add, remove, rename, lock, hide/mute, solo
  - Undo/redo for every change, with a History panel
  - Dark and Light themes, custom themes (import, live editing, export)
  - Resizable panels and saved workspaces
  - Keyboard shortcuts you can search, change, import and export

To run: unzip this folder anywhere and double-click cutline.exe.
Nothing is installed and nothing is sent over the network.

Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher (it isn't
code-signed yet). Choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

Settings, themes and shortcuts are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CUTLINE.
To remove CUTLINE, delete this folder and that one.
